We're Not Racing to Replace Humans with AI

Every firm right now is talking about AI. Most are using it to cut headcount and boost margins. We're using it differently.

AI is great at repetitive tasks: extracting data from PDFs, categorizing transactions, flagging common errors. It's terrible at understanding context: why you structured your business that way, what your actual risk tolerance is, whether a tax position makes sense for your specific situation.

So we use AI to eliminate the tedious work — data entry, document formatting, routine checks. That frees us to do what actually matters: understand your business, anticipate problems before they happen, and explain decisions in plain English. You get the efficiency of automation and the judgment of an experienced professional. Not one or the other.
